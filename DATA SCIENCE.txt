   DATA SCIENCE
Data science is the study of data used to extract meaningful insights for business decisions. It combines mathematics, computing and domain knowledge to solve real-world problems and uncover hidden patterns.

Step Involved:
Data Collection: Gathering raw data from various sources, such as databases, sensors or user interactions.
Data Cleaning: Ensuring the data is accurate, complete and ready for analysis.
Data Analysis: Applying statistical and computational methods to identify patterns, trends or relationships.
Data Visualization: Creating charts, graphs and dashboards to present findings clearly.
Decision-Making: Using insights to inform strategies, create solutions or predict outcomes.

SKILLS:
Programming: Proficiency in programming languages like Python, R or SQL is crucial for analyzing and processing data effectively.
Statistics and Mathematics: A strong foundation in statistics and linear algebra helps in understanding data patterns and building predictive models.
Machine Learning: Knowledge of machine learning algorithms and frameworks is key to creating intelligent data-driven solutions.
Data Visualization: The ability to present data insights through tools like Tableau, Power BI or Matplotlib ensures findings are clear and actionable.
Data Wrangling: Skills in cleaning, transforming and preparing raw data for analysis are vital for maintaining data quality.
Big Data Tools: Familiarity with tools like Hadoop, Spark or cloud platforms helps in handling large datasets efficiently.
Non-Technical Skills: Critical thinking and communication help analyze data effectively and clearly explain insights to 
stakeholders.

APPLICATIONS
Finance & Banking: Used for credit risk modeling, fraudulent transaction detection, and automated algorithmic trading.
Healthcare & Medicine: Powers AI-based medical assessment platforms, diagnostics, and patient risk categorization.
E-commerce & Retail: Powers recommendation engines (personalized shopping suggestions) and sentiment analysis of customer reviews.
Transportation & Logistics: Foundational for self-driving vehicle technology, optimized route planning, and reducing fuel consumption.
Manufacturing: Applied for predictive maintenance of machinery to reduce downtime.
Digital Media & Marketing: Used for audience analytics and optimizing advertisement engagement. 

PROCESS
1.Data collection
2.Data cleaning
3.Data analysis
4.Model building
5.Deployment/Monitoring

ROLES IN Amazon,Swiggy,Meesho
Data science at Amazon is deeply integrated into every aspect of its business, functioning as the core driver for personalized customer experiences, supply chain optimization, and automated decision-making. With a focus on "customer-obsessed science," data scientists at Amazon turn massive datasets into actionable insights to predict demand, set prices, and enhance services.

Swiggy utilizes data science to optimize its entire food delivery ecosystem through machine learning and AI, focusing on personalized customer experiences, efficient logistics, and demand prediction. Key applications include AI-driven ETA models, personalized restaurant recommendations, dynamic pricing, and managing food supply surges during peak times.
